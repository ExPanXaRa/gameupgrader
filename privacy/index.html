Hextrade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Hextrade: Idle Trade Tycoon (“the game”) handles. The game is developed by Game Upgrader (“we”). Contact: unknown.ru.6@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The game has no accounts, no registration, no chat and no analytics. We do not receive, sell or share any information about you.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device, so you can continue playing:

  • game progress (save file);
  • settings (sound, language, graphics);
  • a technical log of game errors, used only on the device to recover after a crash.

This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game. On Android, the system may include the save file in your personal device backup (Android Backup, managed by Google under your account) so that progress is not lost when you change phones. You can turn this off in your device settings.

In the browser version, progress is stored in your browser’s storage or, on game portals, in the portal’s own cloud save (see section 3).

3. Advertising and platform services

The game is free and shows optional rewarded video ads: an ad plays only when you choose to watch it in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by third-party services depending on where you play. These services may process data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system and language, in order to show ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud. They act under their own privacy policies:

  • RuStore version: Yandex Mobile Ads — yandex.com/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement, yandex.com/legal/confidential;
  • Yandex Games version: Yandex Games platform and advertising — yandex.com/legal/confidential;
  • CrazyGames version: CrazyGames platform and advertising — crazygames.com/privacy-policy;
  • Google Play version (when released): Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required by law (for example in the EU/EEA), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Game portals (Yandex Games, CrazyGames) may also provide sign-in, cloud saves and language detection; that data is handled by the portal under its policy.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. Ad partners are instructed to serve only ads appropriate for a general audience.

5. Permissions

On Android the game requests only vibration (for tactile feedback). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

6. Your rights

Since we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to give access to or delete. For data processed by an ad or platform partner, use the rights described in that partner’s policy. If you live in the EU/EEA you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. For any question, write to unknown.ru.6@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds new features. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date.
